About Us

Stories from West Africa, told for the world.

CallTime Productions is a film and commercial production company based in Accra, Ghana. We bring together a team of passionate filmmakers, storytellers, and creatives dedicated to authentic narratives that resonate far beyond our borders.

Who We Are

Founded in Accra, CallTime Productions exists to bridge the gap between local stories and global audiences. We don't just make films we craft cultural conversations.

From narrative features to documentary films, branded content to commercial campaigns, our team brings together international production standards with the rich storytelling traditions of West Africa.

Every project we take on is an opportunity to share the voices, colours, and rhythms of Ghana with the world all while maintaining the highest levels of professional execution and creative integrity.
